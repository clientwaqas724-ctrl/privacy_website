Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Customer Loyalty App. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our applications and services.

By using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Name, email address, phone number, and profile photo
  • Login credentials (email/phone and password)

Business Data

  • Merchant names, contact details, and outlet locations
  • Performance analytics and transaction volumes

Loyalty Data

  • Loyalty points, redemption history, and point expiration
  • Transaction history and coupon usage

Technical Information

  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • App usage data and interaction patterns

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process loyalty points and enable coupon redemptions
  • To send transactional messages and notifications

  • To improve app functionality and user experience
  • To detect and prevent fraud and security breaches

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. Information may be shared in the following circumstances:

All third-party service providers are contractually obligated to protect your data.

5. Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your information:

  • Data encryption in transit and at rest
  • Role-based access controls
  • Secure authentication protocols
  • Regular security assessments

6. Cookies & Tracking

Our services use cookies and similar tracking technologies:

  • Essential cookies for basic functionality
  • Analytical cookies to improve performance
  • Preference cookies for personalized experience

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

7. Push Notifications

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver notifications about:

  • New loyalty points earned
  • Special offers and promotions
  • Coupon expiration reminders
  • Account security alerts

You can manage notification preferences in your device settings.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary:

You may request deletion of your account and data at any time.
